About

I'm a writer, editor, and content strategist with a passion for turning complex ideas into compelling stories. My work spans books, feature journalism, technical documentation, and content marketing. I've written for Wired, The Atlantic, and numerous B2B publications. Whether it's a 280-page narrative non-fiction book or a single landing page that needs to convert, I obsess over the right word in the right place. Good writing is invisible — it carries the reader from idea to action without ever calling attention to itself.
I've written over 2 million words professionally
My first published piece was a letter to the editor at age 12
I own more notebooks than I'll ever fill
